### Thesis
Intel's case is a turnaround on three legs:
1. **AI-driven server CPU demand:** Data Center and AI +59% in Q2; demand still ahead of supply.
2. **A process comeback:** 18A in volume, 14A in development, which needs an external customer to pay for itself.
3. **A refilled balance sheet:** the $20B raise at $95 in August, alongside the government CHIPS arrangement. Q2's GAAP loss
   came mainly from a $12.5B mark-to-market charge on escrowed shares.

The market priced the comeback by June (52-week high $142.35; +150% year to date per TipRanks). Since then it has priced
the *delay*: lower highs from $142 → $127 → $118 → $107 → $97. What the market is missing, if anything, is the customer
announcement. Until one comes, the foundry costs ≈ $2B a quarter.
