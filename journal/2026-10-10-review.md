  proceeds plus most of the Reserve. The plan's rule is at most half the Reserve on any one dip, because the second leg
  is the one that pays. With CPI on Wednesday, the −4% index rung is now worth about $8.
- **The ledger says otherwise.** Every trade made without the desk since 10/2, marked to the 10/9 close against the
  plan's alternative, nets **−$15.43** after fees (PLAN §7). The three new buys are +$12.39, which is two days of noise.
  The QUBT stop lowering alone cost −$14.16.
- **"No internet":** the Amana app trades over the internet, and the plan's levels and locks are written in PLAN §6b and
  on the Flag Board. From now on, if the desk cannot be reached, §6b *is* the desk: "locked" means no trade.
- **"Hang in":** yes. Every line is above its stop, and no thesis broke this week: Samsung posted a record (₩107.4T OP, +0.7%
  vs consensus, 10/8); Bernstein defended WDC/STX (10/5); GTA VI is still 11/19. Holding is the plan. But "hang in" means
  holding with the stops where they were, not lowering them, and not with zero cash.
