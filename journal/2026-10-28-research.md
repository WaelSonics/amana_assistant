## `levels.py`, fixed after the agents' reports (all six flagged the same faults)
- **Recent turns are now listed.** Swing turns use 3 bars each side, and a turn in the last few sessions is listed as
  provisional (\*). The 10/22–10/23 lows (MU 849.25, BE 189.00, AMD 502.60…) were invisible before.
- **More resistance is counted.** The 20/50/200-DMA and the 52-week high now count as levels. Supports that a later close
  broke are dropped.
- **The setup check is stricter:**
  - ⚠ when T1 skips nearer resistance, with the R/R to that level;
  - the levels a rebound must clear are listed;
  - the 2% risk is checked at the zone's high edge;
  - R/R and ATR are rounded down for display, so 1.496 prints as 1.49 ✘, not 1.50;
  - the message for a zone above the price is corrected.
- **Time and calendar:**
  - real Eastern time (zoneinfo), not UTC−4, which matters from 11/1;
  - NYSE holidays in session counts;
  - the blackout window is printed with its dates, plus a note that FOMC days, data mornings, client freezes and caps are not
    checked;
  - EPS estimates are labelled as Alpha Vantage's (often GAAP).
- **Other:**
  - the open intraday bar is flagged;
  - the 200-DMA wording states both rules (the protocol bans longs below a falling one; the desk's 10/24 view also waits
    below a rising one);
  - slopes under 0.25% read "flat";
  - `--fresh` skips the cache.

Known limits that stay:
- the blackout counts the report day for both before-open and after-close reports (conservative; matches the PLAN's AMD and
  LITE windows);
- `--stop` takes a fixed price only;
- Alpha Vantage misses monthly reporters (PGR) and some ADRs.
