## What it says
- **Perfectly followed, v3 does not beat the index.** In 20 years it lost $11–21k to it, and its spread across stock lists
  is luck.
- **50/50 loses because of cash, not stock picks.** A disciplined trading half sits in cash about ¾ of the time; keeping
  it invested means buying without setups, which is the override pattern.
- **v4 is the S&P within ±$4k,** plus a capped test of the rules.
- **The real gap is behavior:** the fill pace and the overrides, not the rules. That is why §10 test 1 is zero overrides.

## Corrections made during the study
- **An early projection put trading at 3%/yr.** That figure was too harsh on the rules as written; followed perfectly,
  the rules roughly match the index.
- **v4's pot-close rule was changed.** It was "pot falls to 10% of the account"; it is now "pot loses half its starting
  value". The old rule would have closed the pot without any loss, just from VOO deposits growing the account.
