# Plan change — v2 active-trading rules — 2026-10-01

**Request (client):** loosen the rules a bit; physical gold + cash savings outside the account are the real safety
layer, so allow more trading while keeping strict rules. Always show a dip-buy zone for every holding on the artifacts.

**Desk objection, stated once:** the data argues for discipline, not volume. Pre-reset: 112 fills, +$97 realised,
$75 in fees. Post-reset: 20 fills, +$317. And the binding limit today is cash, not rules: the account is ~91% invested.
Loosening rules does not create buying power; the $200 a month and profit trims do. 8 fills a month costs up to $96 a
year (~2.6% of the sleeve).

**Decision:** the client owns the plan and the vault does cover the downside. v2 adopted (see PLAN.md §1b):
fills ≤ 8/month · Reserve floor 10% · 6 satellites · one speculative slot ≤ 6% at cost · 2 adds per name (reset after a
profit-banking sale), each ≤ $150 · earnings blackout 5 trading days · VOO −4% rung · every holding always has a zone.
Unchanged: stop + R/R ≥ 2:1 before every buy, zones above stops, hard exits, 15% name cap, trim ladder, circuit breaker,
no leverage / shorts / options / CFDs / crypto.

**Effect on 10/1 recommendations:** QUBT is no longer a forced sale (fits the speculative slot). Oct $200 still goes to
the Reserve (8.8% < 10%). Dip zones unlock once cash is above 10%: ≈ $135 to spend after the contribution.

**Review 2026-12-31:** compare fills beyond the old 2/month pace, net of fees. If negative, revert to v1 numbers.
