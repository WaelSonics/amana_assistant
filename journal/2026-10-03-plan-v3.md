## Decision — plan v3 (PLAN.md §1b, §3b)
Loosened: ≤ 10 buys a month with sells uncounted (was 8 fills) · Reserve floor 7.5% (was 10%) · **trade-around**: up to
⅓ of any satellite sold in its written sell zone and bought back in its buy zone, repeatable · one-fill entries up to
8% · earnings blackout 3 sessions (was 5) · banked profit refills the Reserve to 15% before the 50/50 split · satellite
band 45–60%.
New guards: risk per buy ≤ 2% of the sleeve · open stop-risk ≤ 8% (today 7.1%) · $50 minimum buy · `TA` tags ·
a thesis headline locks adds until the next earnings data point · §4a re-entry moved into PLAN.md, wait loosened from one
  week to the next session (still needs evidence + new stop + R/R ≥ 2:1).
Kept: stop + R/R ≥ 2:1 before every buy, chase guard, 15/20% name caps, ratchet, circuit breaker, 6 satellites,
spec slot ≤ 6%, 2 adds per holding period, no leverage/shorts/options/CFDs/crypto, vault untouched.
**Why these and not others:** the chase guard is tied to the two worst non-short losses (SNDK −$80, BE −$63). R/R and
stops are what make a trade non-random. Fees took ≈ 30% of pre-reset profit, hence the buy cap and the $50 minimum.
