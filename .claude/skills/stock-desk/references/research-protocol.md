# Research protocol — per-ticker deep dive

Run every section. Cite the date of every number. Save the result as `research/TICKER-YYYY-MM-DD.md`
using the "Ticker deep dive" template in `output-templates.md`.

## 0. Freshness sweep (do first, WebSearch)
- "TICKER stock news" restricted to the last 7 days; "TICKER earnings date"; "TICKER guidance";
  "TICKER analyst downgrade/upgrade" last 30 days; "TICKER SEC 8-K" last 30 days.
- Note the **next earnings date** and the days until it. Inside 3 trading sessions: no buy (PLAN §1b).
  Inside 10: smaller size or wait, and state the expected gap — gap risk on a small account is real.

## 1. Chart and technicals
Get current price, 52-week range, and the following (search "TICKER technical analysis" or read a
chart page; state the source and date):
- **Trend structure**: higher highs/lows or lower? Position vs 20/50/200-day moving averages.
  Above rising 50 & 200 = trend long; below falling 200 = no fresh longs, only mean-reversion trims.
- **Key levels**: nearest support (prior swing low, gap fill, 50/200 DMA) and resistance
  (prior high, gap, round number). These become entry zone and invalidation.
- **Momentum**: RSI(14) — >70 extended (don't chase; wait for pullback), <30 washed out (look for
  base, not falling knife). MACD direction.
- **Volume**: was the recent move on rising volume (conviction) or fading volume (exhaustion)?
- **Volatility**: ATR(14) or recent daily range in %. Sets the stop distance — stop must sit
  beyond ~1.5× ATR from entry or it will be noise-stopped.
- **Distance from the 50-DMA in %**: >20% above = crowded/extended for a new entry.

## 2. Fundamentals
- Revenue growth (YoY, last quarter and guided), gross margin trend, operating margin, FCF sign.
- Valuation: forward P/E or EV/Sales vs own 3-year range and vs peers. Is the story priced in?
- Balance sheet: net cash/debt, dilution history (share count trend), any convertibles.
- Quality of earnings: one-off items, stock comp as % of revenue.
- For pre-profit names (BE-type): path to FCF breakeven, backlog/bookings, customer concentration.

## 3. News and catalysts
- Last 30 days: contracts, guidance changes, management changes, regulatory/policy, competitor moves.
- Upcoming: earnings, product events, policy decisions, index inclusion, lockup expiries.
- Classify each as thesis-confirming, thesis-neutral, thesis-breaking.

## 4. Positioning and sentiment
- Short interest and days-to-cover if available; recent insider buying/selling; analyst consensus
  and recent revisions; whether the name is a "crowded AI trade" in current commentary.
- Crowded + extended + earnings soon = the setup that produces the sharp crashes the client wants to avoid.

## 5. Thesis, invalidation, reward/risk
- One-paragraph thesis: why this stock, why now, what the market is missing or under-weighting.
- **Invalidation**: a price level *and* a fundamental event, either of which exits the position.
- **Target**: technical (measured move/prior high) and fundamental (valuation re-rate) — use the lower.
- **R/R** = (target − entry) / (entry − stop). Recommend only if ≥ 2.0.

## 6. Verdict
Fill the verdict table. Choose exactly one action: BUY / ADD / HOLD / TRIM / EXIT / WATCH (with the
level that turns WATCH into BUY). Conviction: High / Medium / Low with one line why.
